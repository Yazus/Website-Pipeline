Why forty grams

We mill your tin the day you order and post it the next morning. UK delivery is free over £40. Choose a tin to write to us.

  1. Sift two grams.

    Two level scoops of the bamboo chashaku, through a fine sieve into a warm bowl. It stops the lumps.

  2. Pour 70 ml at 80°.

    Water just off the boil, left a minute to settle. Hotter than that scalds the tea bitter.

  3. Whisk a quick M.

    Fifteen seconds from the wrist, not the arm, until a fine foam closes over the surface.

  4. Drink it green.

    Within a few minutes of whisking, and the tin within a month of the date on its lid.
